# Business Sales Performance Analytics

## 📌 Project Overview
This project analyzes business sales data to understand revenue trends, profitability, and performance across products, categories, and regions. The goal is to generate actionable insights that help businesses make data-driven decisions.

This project was completed as part of **Data Science & Analytics – Task 1** by **Future Interns**.

---

## 🎯 Objectives
- Identify top revenue-generating products  
- Analyze sales trends over time  
- Evaluate profitability by category and region  
- Provide actionable business recommendations  

---

## 📊 Dataset
- **Sample – Superstore Dataset**
- Contains sales, profit, product, category, region, and date information
- Represents a real-world retail business scenario

---

## 🛠 Tools Used
- **Tableau Public** – Data visualization and dashboard creation  

---

## 📈 Key Insights
- Technology category generates the highest profit  
- Sales peak towards the end of the year, indicating seasonal demand  
- A small number of products contribute significantly to overall revenue  
- The West region is the most profitable  

---

## 💡 Business Recommendations
- Focus inventory and promotions on top-performing products  
- Invest more in high-margin categories such as Technology  
- Improve operational strategies in low-performing regions  
- Leverage seasonal trends for marketing campaigns
